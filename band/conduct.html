Twinkle Variations

Beat time with the baton: the players follow your downbeats, and the size of your beat is how loud they play. Point at a section and hold the pinch to bring it forward; raise or lower the arm while holding to balance it. Cue each entrance marked on the timeline, and pinch the last chord away.
