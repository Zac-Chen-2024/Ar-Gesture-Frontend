XB Lab · logo sketches

Six marks

1Swipe

The gesture you would draw on our keyboard to type “xblab”: X → B → L → A → B in one stroke. The lab's name is its own input. It draws itself, so it is ready-made for the transition.

2Crossing

Two lines, one for people and one for machines, cross to make the X. The dot where they meet is interaction itself, and that is where HCI happens.

3Keycap

A single key with XB printed on it and a swipe crossing it. It says text entry plainly and works well as a small square icon.

4Ripple

The bowls of the B spread like ripples from a touch, and the X marks where the finger lands. It reads as touch and tap.

5Caret

A blinking text cursor sits between X and B, so the lab is the place where text gets entered. It is the plainest and most typographic of the six.

6Loop

The interaction loop runs from the person to the system and back to the person, drawn as a figure eight. The crossing at its centre is the X: people on the left, the computer on the right.
