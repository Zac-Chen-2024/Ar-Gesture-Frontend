Loop

Infinity, standing up

Lying down it is ∞, the loop of interaction: people act, the computer answers, people act again. Where the two loops cross there is an X. Stand the loop up and straighten its left side, and it is a B.

The upper loop is the person, in ink. The lower loop is the computer, in the mode's colour. The dot is the moment they meet, the same dot that ends every swipe on our keyboard.

X crosses → grows into ∞ → stands up → leans left and its left side straightens into B → XBLab slides out. Click to replay.
