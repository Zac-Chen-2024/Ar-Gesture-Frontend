01

Motion

An X is crossed. Its arms run on into ∞, the loop of interaction: people act, the computer answers. The loop stands up, and a mirror line runs out of the X and splits it into two Bs, back to back. Then the name follows.

02

Mark

Two loops, each a circle with two tangents that meet at the crossing. The upper loop is the person, in ink; the lower loop is the computer, in the mode's colour. The dot is where they meet, the same dot that ends every swipe on our keyboard.

03

Variants

Use the two-colour mark wherever colour is available. Use one colour for print and stamps, and the reversed mark on dark or coloured grounds.

04

With the name

The name's capitals are half the mark's height, centred on it, one loop's width away. The descriptor is set in small spaced capitals and never sits closer to the name than the mark does.

05

Small sizes

Below 48 px the line gets heavier and the dot is dropped, so the mark stays open at small sizes.
