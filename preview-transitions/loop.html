Loop

Infinity, standing up

Lying down it is ∞, the loop of interaction: people act, the computer answers, people act again. Where the two loops cross there is an X. Stand it up and let it lean left: the crossing slides onto a straight stem and it is a B.

The upper loop is the person, in ink. The lower loop is the computer, in the mode's colour. The dot is the moment they meet, the same dot that ends every swipe on our keyboard.

X crosses → grows into ∞ → stands up → leans left until the crossing sits on a straight stem: B → XBLab slides out. Click to replay.
