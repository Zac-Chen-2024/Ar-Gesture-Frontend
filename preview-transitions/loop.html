Loop

Infinity, in a mirror

Lying down it is ∞, the loop of interaction: people act, the computer answers, people act again. Where the two loops cross there is an X.

Stand it up and set a mirror through the crossing: the line splits the loop into two Bs, back to back, one the reflection of the other, the person and the computer. The X stays where all four curves meet the mirror.

X crosses → grows into ∞ → stands up → a mirror line runs out of the X and the two Bs take shape → XBLab slides out. Click to replay.
